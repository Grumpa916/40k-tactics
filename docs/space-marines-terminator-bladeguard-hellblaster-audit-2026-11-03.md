# Space Marines 11th edition wargear audit: Terminator Squad, Bladeguard, Hellblasters

Date: 2026-11-03. **Scope:** three additional Space Marines datasheets, using current 11th-edition reference comparisons, not a full independent transcription of the physical October 2026 Codex or the entire official Munitorum Field Manual.

## Authority and source traceability

- Games Workshop [Warhammer 40,000 Downloads](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/downloads/warhammer-40000/) identifies the latest **Munitorum Field Manual updated October 14, 2026**. Its official rules and cost documents remain definitive.
- Games Workshop [Big Space Marines News](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/bbnskauh/big-space-marines-news/) confirms the 11th-edition Terminator T6 and S5 Storm Bolter baseline and Tacticus T5 upgrade.
- Current 11e independent datasheet comparisons: [Terminator Squad](https://wh-rules.ru/factions/space-marines/datasheets/terminator-squad), [Bladeguard Veteran Squad](https://www.wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/space-marines/Bladeguard-Veteran-Squad), [Hellblaster Squad](https://wh-rules.ru/factions/space-marines/datasheets/hellblaster-squad). These references supply detailed equipment, weapon and additional-copy values. They are **not** an independently accessed current Games Workshop Codex PDF or Warhammer App unit record.

## Selected loadouts implemented

| Unit | Model options | Selectable equipment | Combat reference update |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terminator Squad | 5 or 10 | Sergeant Power Fist / Chainfist / Power Weapon; regular members Power Fist / Chainfist. Regular members can take Assault Cannon, Heavy Flamer, or Cyclone Missile Launcher **plus Storm Bolter**, shared limit one heavy choice per 5 models | T6, Storm Bolter S5 AP-1, Assault Cannon Sustained Hits 1, Heavy Flamer Blast 2, Cyclone Frag/Krak profiles and Chainfist Standard/Hunter profiles |
| Bladeguard Veteran Squad | 3 or 6 | Sergeant Heavy Bolt Pistol / Neo-volkite Pistol / Plasma Pistol; all retain their Master-crafted Power Swords | T5, 4+ invulnerable, updated Power Sword and pistols; Bladeguard Fight-phase offensive/defensive choice shown as a reminder |
| Hellblaster Squad | 5 or 10 | Sergeant Bolt Pistol / Plasma Pistol; all retain Plasma Incinerators and Ceramite Fists | Plasma Incinerator Standard/Supercharge with Rapid Fire 1 and Damage 2; S5 Bolt Pistol, Rites of Thermal Appeasement reminder |

**Weapon-mode distinction:** Cyclone Frag/Krak, Chainfist Standard/Hunter, Plasma Incinerator Standard/Supercharge and Plasma Pistol Standard/Supercharge are listed as **alternate attack profiles**, not separate physical weapons. The user selects appropriate attacks when resolving combat. Hunter target eligibility, Hazardous rolls, and timing effects remain player-confirmed.

## Points and limits

- Terminator Squad 5/10 models: **190/380** for first two copies, **230/420** for third and later. Each selected Cyclone Missile Launcher adds **10 army points**. Its Storm Bolter is retained.
- Bladeguard Veteran Squad uses already reviewed **90/180** first two and **110/200** third-and-later costs.
- Hellblasters: **115/230** for first two, **130/245** for third-and-later copies.
- The new combined cap prevents mixing Assault Cannons, Heavy Flamers and Cyclone Launchers above the total **one per five Terminator models** limit.
- The roster price validator includes Cyclone upgrades rather than incorrectly comparing only the base MFM row. **No saved roster is silently altered.**

## UI and safety

- The shared equipment editor can now display both a unique Sergeant slot and individual regular-member groups on the same datasheet; it preserves the existing all-per-model screens for other factions.
- Readiness remains strict about equipment selection. Third-party transcriptions are marked with the scoped review date; no complete current official Codex certification is asserted.
- Existing gameplay, scoring, terrain, primary/secondary objectives, and saved game data are unchanged.
