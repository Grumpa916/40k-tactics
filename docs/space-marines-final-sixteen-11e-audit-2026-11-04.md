## Source and certification boundary

Every entry below was cross-checked against a current 11th-edition *independent* datasheet transcription at `https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/space-marines/<datasheet>`. These are current 11e equipment/profiles and gameplay notes, **not a declaration of certification against the full Games Workshop October 2026 Codex or the official live Munitorum Field Manual**. Existing points were retained because all 16 one-model prices match the cross-checked current 11e reference. Confirm official source revisions at [GW Downloads](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/downloads/warhammer-40000/) and [MFM](https://mfm.warhammer-community.com/en/space-marines) before tournament use.

## Data-model integration

- **Existing catalog IDs preserved.** The 12 named Epic Heroes that previously had only a name/points candidate receive independent 11e source-scoped characteristics, weapons and ability reminders. The older imported first-four characters retain their ID and receive corrected 11e profiles.
- **No invented Legends/old-Codex data.** The reviewed-profile overlay may construct a missing datasheet only for individually reviewed characters (and previously reviewed Captain on Bike); all unrelated missing datasheets remain unresolved. The Legends index is separate and still displays the appropriate pending verification warning.
- **Legal equipment:** 15 characters have only fixed wargear. Phobos Lieutenant has a precise two-choice swap. A Combi-weapon's Bolter/Flamer, Severance and Rebuke's Strike/Sweep, and Hand of Dominion's ranged/melee modes are **one equipped weapon each**, not multiple purchases or guaranteed extra melee activations.
- **Safe import behavior:** Fixed-only reviewed characters may hydrate empty-equipment Warhammer App imports; explicit, unmatched or source-invalid import selections are never silently replaced. Phobos Lieutenant must be manually confirmed and is excluded from fixed-only hydration.
- **Live play remains manual:** No automatic changes to CP, VP, wounds, psychic resources, unit movement, battleshock, objective status, detachment or Warlord choice. All timing, selection, target, doctrinal and saving constraints are written as ability reminders to aid the Tactical Advisor without overwriting recorded game state.
- **Points:** Current source checks match catalog 1-model values; no new guesswork or auto repricing of saved rosters.
- **Test revision:** `2026-11-04.19`, expected **87 / 87 source-reviewed** current non-Legends unit types, `83` combat-profile overlays plus four earlier independently reviewed Ultramarines entries. Regression suite checks all 16 plus full catalog, and verifies unreviewed Legends still flagged.
