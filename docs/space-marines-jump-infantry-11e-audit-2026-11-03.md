# 11e Space Marines Jump Pack infantry review
Reviewed November 3, 2026 US Pacific. Current 11th-edition datasheet **transcription** cross-check, not an assertion that the full October printed GW Codex or GW MFM was directly page-certified.

## Implemented
| Unit | Builder/model-specific rules | Reference/combat and cost |
|---|---|---|
| Assault Intercessors with Jump Packs | 5/10 models; fixed Chainsword on ordinary models; Sergeant only can take Power Fist/Power Weapon, Hand Flamer or separately labeled Sergeant Plasma Pistol; max one regular model Plasma Pistol per five; Standard/Supercharge share one gun. | M12 T5 W2 OC1; S5 Chainsword/Heavy Bolt Pistol; manual Hammer of Wrath charge reminder. 5/10 first-two prices 95/190, third+ 105/200. |
| Vanguard Veteran Squad with Jump Packs | 5/10 models; ordinary Veterans retain Master-crafted Power Weapon; Sergeant Relic Blade can swap to Power Fist/Thunder Hammer; Sergeant pistol choice independent of two regular-model Plasma Pistols per five; every model can swap Heavy Bolt Pistol for Combat Shield, never receive both on same model. Shield is **not** a ranged weapon and grants only the equipped model 5+ invulnerable save. | M12 T5 W2 OC1; current Relic Blade/Cleave and charge-dependent +1 melee Attack. Existing MFM 110/220 first-two and 130/240 third+ retained. |

**Source trap:** Exclude obsolete 10th-edition Vanguard Storm Shields, Grav-pistols, Hand Flamers, Inferno Pistols and Vanguard Veteran weapon profiles; Combat Shield on 11e is a different selectable item.

**Conservative UI modeling:** "Sergeant Plasma Pistol" is a UI label for the Sergeant-only pistol; the underlying combat profiles remain the same Plasma Pistol Standard/Supercharge. This prevents imports and mixed-model counts from silently exceeding the legal regular-Veteran limit. Older saved games, manual VP/CP/scoring, approved terrain/maps and V1 are unchanged. Manual importer review is required for ambiguous source selections. Do not auto-apply the shield's invulnerable save to all models or Hammer of Wrath rolls.
