### Price discrepancy and safety
Wahapedia and 40k.app currently show **80** for 3 Outriders, while Praetorium's October page lists **85**; both show **160** for six. Preserve the pre-existing 80 and explicitly leave official MFM confirmation open; do not silently reprice saved lists.
