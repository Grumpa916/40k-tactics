# 11th Edition Space Marines — 10-unit continuation audit (November 4, 2026)

**Scope:** Drop Pod, Impulsor, Rhino, Land Speeder, Storm Speeder Hailstrike, Storm Speeder Hammerstrike, Storm Speeder Thunderstrike, Astraeus, Thunderhawk Gunship, Techmarine. These 10 entries exist in the current non-Legends app catalog. Razorback, Stormhawk Interceptor and Stormraven Gunship are Legends entries in the current 11e reference and are **not** substituted into the normal current catalog.

**Evidence:** independent, contemporaneous 11e datasheet transcriptions at `https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/space-marines/{Unit-Slug}`, including the current Space Marines index. **Not** a direct page-by-page certificate of the GW October 2026 Codex, Forge World supplements or official live MFM: `https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/downloads/warhammer-40000/`, `https://mfm.warhammer-community.com/en/space-marines`. The accuracy status is *source-scoped cross-check* only.

## Scope, correctness and limitations
- No old edition optional wargear is restored. An equipped missile array contributes *one weapon* even with three alternate firing profiles.
- Combat-profile overlays are isolated from the scraped community-reference projection, maintain explicit verification flags, and do not rewrite imported point snapshots or saved player rosters.
- Old saved/loadout imports with unknown or conflicting weapons remain unresolved until the player chooses and saves a reviewed legal loadout; fixed-only hydrations are restricted to four unambiguous items (the three fixed Storm Speeders and Techmarine).
- The 4th+ transport pricing is explicitly annotated, rather than misusing the pre-existing 3rd+ tier helper. Orbital Comms is the only priced Impulsor roof upgrade; its Shield Dome and other options are not billed as additional points.
- Drop Pod battle-readiness is special-cased because it genuinely has no equipped combat weapons. Existing source-invalid states still block. All actual wounds, CP, VP, secondary action scoring, map placement and terrain ownership remain manual and unmodified.
- **Outstanding:** direct Games Workshop October Codex and Forge World/MFM comparison; Astraeus stubber inventory; Thunderhawk cluster-bomb swap; mixed/complex transports in live iPad Safari; once-used missiles and after-shooting debuffs in the tactical advisor; saved roster reconciliation. This audit alone does not certify every rule implementation or every 11e army entry.
- Tests cover each of the ten ids, legal/illegal selections, no-equipment readiness, no phantom Comms/Shield attacks, paired Astraeus guns, one-shot equipment, conditional shooting bonuses, vehicle/character statistics, and all revised points tiers.
